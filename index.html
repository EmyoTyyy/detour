How to play

  • Each turn: move your pawn one step, or place a wall.
  • First pawn to reach the opposite row wins — or in Race, where you both start on the same side, the shared top row. In King of the hill it's the single ringed cell in the centre instead.
  • Walls go between cells and block movement. You start with 10. Drag one onto a junction, or click one and then click a junction.
  • A wall can't fully trap a player — both must keep a path home.
  • Jump a face-to-face opponent; side-step if a wall is behind them.
  • The graph button in a bot or local game turns on Path, the built-in engine: a bar showing who is winning, its best move drawn straight away as a green arrow or ghost wall, and a verdict on every move you play — brilliant, best, book, inaccuracy, mistake or blunder.
  • After a game, hit Game review to replay it move by move with Path's move marked in green on every position, see where it turned, and try to find the best move yourself. Past games are kept under Game history.
